# Benchmark evidence

The authoritative campaign used by the revised article is:

- `revision-2026-09-29/` — 20 fresh processes per reported row, one warmup, at most four externally granted participants, with raw data, retained data, machine/configuration records, outlier sensitivity, aggregate statistics, and report.
- `independent-dedicated-100/` — independent follow-up validation on a bare-metal AMD Ryzen host, with 100 fresh processes per row after three warmups. It is retained as corroborating evidence and does not replace any numerical value printed in the article.

Older `primary/` and `scaling/` snapshots were removed during the reviewer revision because they were produced under superseded environments and could contradict the article's revised numbers.

GitHub Actions also runs a fresh publication-scale campaign on every push/PR. Those host-specific measurements are uploaded as one-day workflow artifacts and are not committed as replacement article data.
